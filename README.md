## About me

I am a mechanical engineering researcher working at the intersection of **intelligent manufacturing, additive manufacturing, machine learning, and computational geometry**.

My research combines experimental platform development, computer vision, predictive modeling, and inverse design to improve manufacturing processes. During my doctoral research at the University of Guelph, I developed experimental manufacturing and monitoring systems and investigated data-driven process optimization and additively manufactured structured catalysts. My current postdoctoral work focuses on computational approaches to digital engineering and manufacturing.

This profile highlights selected research repositories, earlier engineering projects, video demonstrations, and publications. Some research software remains private while the related manuscripts are prepared for publication.
